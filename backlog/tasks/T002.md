  blob — see spec trade-off 9 for why)
- `src/devtools/fixtures.ts` — seeded, deterministic fixtures per spec §11 (empty / 1-month / 3-year /
  cap-exceeded / budget-blown ledgers) plus a TimeTravel control for the date the app derives against
- Unit tests on `plotFromIndex` (i = 0..23, per spec §5 AC — building at index 6 directly below index
  5, serpentine adjacency) and on every selector
