**F6 · Monthly budget + town mood**: one global monthly budget (KRW), editable from 기록/설정.
`elapsedFraction = dayOfMonth / daysInMonth`, `expectedSpend = budget × elapsedFraction`,
`pace = monthExpenseTotal / expectedSpend` (guard `expectedSpend > 0` — already in `budgetPace`).
Pace buckets into 3 mood tiers via `moodTier`. Mood drives: (a) the S2 sky gradient, (b) a one-line
status in the town header, (c) the budget-bar color in 기록 (already done). If `budget === null`,
mood is pinned neutral and the header nudges "예산을 정하면 우리 동네 날씨가 생겨요".
AC: day 15 of a 30-day month, 600,000원 budget, 300,000원 spent → pace = 1.0. Mood responds on day 3
of the month, not only at month end (i.e. it's a continuous function of elapsed time, not a
month-end-only calculation). Mood **never** removes, greys out, or downgrades a building — it's
ambient (sky/header/bar color) only, never touches building state. Month rollover resets month
totals and therefore mood.
