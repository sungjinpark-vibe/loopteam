## Brief
Design ADDENDUM-03-monetization.md. The director gave three concrete mechanics (Discord/in-session,
2026-08-05) — treat these as the brief's core, do not soften or genericize them:

1. **Ads, not banner/interstitial spam.** A built building shows a speech bubble roughly every 10
   minutes. Tapping the bubble plays a rewarded ad and pays out an in-game currency reward.
2. **Paid extra construction.** Past the daily cap (now 10/day, `dailyBuildSlots` per T016), one
   additional build costs ₩1,000 (KRW), real money. Design whether this is per-additional-build
   (repeatable, no upper bound stated by the director) or capped, and surface it as an explicit open
   decision if the director didn't say.
3. **Decoration shop.** Village/building cosmetic items, ₩100–₩5,000 range. Must be purchasable BOTH
   with the new in-game currency (from mechanic 1) AND with real money.

**This explicitly reverses a deliberate MVP-SPEC.md design decision** (§1.3's cut-decisions table:
"Coins as currency — CUT entirely — Removing currency removes the perverse farm loop by construction:
there is nothing to farm by logging more spending," plus §8's rule 2, "Game-side quantities are never
rendered in monetary format... stops a future 'coins' feature from making a finance super-app's
mini-app look like it holds a fake balance," plus the MoSCoW note "Deliberately excluded from MVP:
currency, shops, cosmetic purchases... ads"). That reasoning was sound for the original MVP scope; the
director has now made an explicit, informed monetization decision (this is D-7's answer, previously
open) that supersedes it. **Do not re-litigate the reversal** — design the new economy well, but do
flag anywhere the new currency risks the exact failure mode the original cut was protecting against
(e.g., an entry field or UI surface where a game-currency number could be visually confused with a
real KRW amount) and how your design avoids it, since that specific risk is still real and was the
whole reason for the original rule.

**Platform constraint — read before designing the ad/payment mechanics themselves**: per
`app_in_toss/VISION.md` and MVP-SPEC.md §10.3, real rewarded-ad SDK access and real IAP/Toss Pay both
require Toss developer-console registration and business registration, which the director has stated
are **not yet provided** ("계정이랑 필요한 것들은 추후에 추가할게"). Design this the same way every
other platform touchpoint in this codebase already works: a `src/platform/ads` port and extending
`src/platform/*` for payment, each with a **browser/dev driver today** (ad tap = instant reward with a
short "광고 시청 중..." stub delay, no real ad network call; ₩ purchases = a dev-only mock purchase
flow that completes locally) and a **toss driver later** once accounts exist. §10.2 already documents
this port pattern for storage/clock/haptics/insets/analytics — follow it, don't invent a different
shape. This means the full economy (currency balance, shop, extra-build purchase flow, ad-bubble
timer/UI) can be fully built and demoed today; only the real ad revenue and real payment settlement
are blocked on accounts.

**Design questions to resolve** (do not leave implicit):
- What is the in-game currency called, and how is its balance shown without visually reading as real
  money (ties to the risk flagged above)?
- Ad-bubble timing: exactly which built buildings are eligible (all of them? one at a time? does
  claiming one reset all others' timers or just that one's?), what happens if the player doesn't tap
  it (does it expire, stack, or wait indefinitely), and how does this interact with `TimeTravel`
  (§11.B) for QA testability — the ~10-minute timer must derive from the `clock` port like everything
  else, not `setInterval`/real wall-clock, per §10.2's lint rule.
- Extra-build purchase: exact flow (confirm dialog before spending real money — this is a hard
  UX/compliance expectation, not optional), what happens to a purchased build relative to the existing
  materials queue (F14) and the queued-entry mechanic.
- Decoration shop: what decorations exist for MVP (a small starter catalog, not 40 items — scope this
  honestly per P4), how do they attach to buildings/village (visual-only, confirm no gameplay effect),
  catalog structure, dual-currency pricing display.
- Data model additions needed (new `TownState` fields, storage schema version bump if needed — check
  `LAYOUT_VERSION`/similar existing migration precedent before inventing a new one).
