**Shipped defaults from the 7 open decisions (D-33..D-39, director-reviewed 2026-08-04)** — build to
these, they are not still open:
- D-33: moving is free and unlimited (no slot cost, no daily cap, no cooldown)
- D-34: tapping an occupied lot is rejected (not a swap)
- D-35: 무지출 공원 (park) buildings are movable like everything else
- D-36: the discoverability hint mechanism is required; the copy is a placeholder the director may
  edit later — do not treat the exact Korean wording as final, just ship something reasonable
- D-37: a building may only move among lots already open (not into ground the town hasn't grown into)
- D-38: (if not already resolved by T009) uniform random placement, not street-front-biased
- D-39: a new MVP-SPEC §7 design invariant is proposed ("the town's area is a function of how many
  buildings you have earned, never of where they are put") — note this in your report; do not edit
  MVP-SPEC.md, the PM handles the merge

**Interaction summary** (full detail in §4): 500ms long-press on a building → move mode (the building
is marked `.town-tile--moving`, every free lot in the open pool glows as `.town-tile--droppable`) →
tap a droppable lot → the building moves there, with a 되돌리기 (undo) affordance for a few seconds
→ tap elsewhere or Escape/back cancels move mode without moving anything. One delegated listener on
`.town-grid` (not per-tile handlers — the town can have thousands of tiles), keyboard-accessible
(arrow keys + Enter/Escape), one tab stop for the whole grid regardless of town size.
